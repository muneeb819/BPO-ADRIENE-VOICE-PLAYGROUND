How it works

From dialer to reviewed conversation in four steps.

2

Compliance & safety

Guardrails are product features, not footnotes.

Consent before transfer

A transfer is only attempted after the caller agrees and the policy allows it. The backend is the authority.

Call recording policy

Audio retention is a configured number of days, visible in the admin panel and adjustable by an operator.

Human handoff

The bot stops speaking the moment a caller is transferred, and the event is written to the session timeline.

FAQ

Questions we get asked first.

Which languages can the agents handle?

English is the tested path today. The voice catalogue, the transcription models and the prompt are configurable per agent, so additional languages are a configuration change rather than a rebuild — but only ship what you have actually tested.

Do we need to give you our customer data?

No. The platform runs on your own infrastructure and keeps provider keys server-side. Nothing about a call is sent to us.

How does a human agent take over a call?

The agent requests a transfer, the caller is asked for consent, and only then is the handoff made. The transcript, intent and consent state travel with the call.

Can we review what the bot said afterwards?

Yes. Every turn is stored with its transcript, latency and intent, and the admin panel lets you replay a session or export a regression run.

What happens if a provider is down?

The failure is surfaced, not hidden. The admin panel shows adapter health, and the browser fallbacks keep the call alive while the issue is investigated.

How long does a rollout take?

Start with one campaign and one use case. Agent configurations are versioned, so a rollback is a single action, and you can widen the scope once the transcripts look right.
