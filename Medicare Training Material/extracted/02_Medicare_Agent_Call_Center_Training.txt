## Slide 4
Core Compliance Rules
Non-negotiable requirements for every marketing / sales / enrollment call
Identify Fully
State full name, licensed agent status, agency name. Disclose you are not affiliated with Medicare or the government.
Recording Consent
Advise the call is being recorded and obtain clear verbal permission before continuing.
TPMO Disclaimer
Deliver the required disclaimer about the number of organizations / plans you represent before discussing benefits.
Scope of Appointment
Document product types the beneficiary wants to discuss before any plan-specific conversation.
No Cold Calling
Only contact beneficiaries with documented prior permission. Never use purchased lists without consent.
No Pressure Language
Avoid “best,” “free,” false urgency, or any implication of government endorsement.

## Slide 6
Step 1 — Opening & Required Disclosures
SAMPLE SCRIPT
“Hello, may I speak with [Prospect Name]?
Hi [Name], this is [Your Full Name], a licensed insurance agent with [Agency Name]. This call may be recorded for quality assurance and compliance purposes. Do I have your permission to continue and record this conversation?
[Wait for clear yes.]
Thank you. I am calling regarding Medicare plan options. I represent [list carriers / number of organizations]. I am not connected with or endorsed by the federal government or the Medicare program. Is it okay to proceed?”

## Slide 7
Step 2 — Scope of Appointment (SOA)
Must be completed and documented before any plan-specific discussion.
SAMPLE SOA LANGUAGE
“Before we discuss any specific plans, I need to document the types of products you would like to review today. This is called a Scope of Appointment.
Would you like to discuss Medicare Advantage plans, Medicare Supplement plans, Part D Prescription Drug plans, or a combination?
Please note that the products we discuss today will be limited to what you authorize right now. You are under no obligation to enroll in anything.”
Limit Scope
Stay strictly within the product types authorized.
Document It
Verbal or electronic record is required.
No Obligation
Always remind the beneficiary there is no pressure to enroll.

## Slide 9
Step 4 — Needs Discovery
Listen more than you talk. These questions uncover what truly matters.
1
Current Coverage
What type of coverage do you have now? (Original + Supplement, MA, Part D, etc.)
2
Satisfaction
How happy are you with your current plan? Any costs, coverage gaps, or access issues?
3
Providers
Which doctors, specialists, or hospitals are most important for you to keep?
4
Medications
What prescription medications do you take regularly?
5
Priorities
What matters most — lower premiums, predictable costs, dental/vision, broader network?
6
Upcoming Needs
Any upcoming procedures or expected changes in health needs?

## Slide 10
Steps 5–7 — Recap, Present, Close
5. Recap
•  Summarize doctors, meds, priorities, and budget concerns
•  Ask: “Did I get that right? Anything missing?”
•  Builds trust and prevents mis-matches
6. Present Options
•  Stay within the SOA products only
•  Present 1–3 relevant options factually
•  State trade-offs clearly (network, prior auth, costs)
•  Remind: “I do not offer every plan in your area”
7. Close / Next Steps
•  If ready: confirm understanding, complete enrollment
•  If not: offer summary + schedule follow-up
•  Never pressure same-call enrollment
•  End with contact info + Medicare.gov / 1-800-MEDICARE

## Slide 11
Do’s and Don’ts Quick Reference
DO
✓  Sound helpful and educational
✓  Document every required step
✓  Verify network & formulary fit first
✓  Offer Medicare.gov / SHIP as alternatives
✓  Recap needs before presenting options
✓  Stay within the agreed SOA
✓  End every call professionally
DON’T
✗  Cold call or use unpermitted lists
✗  Discuss products outside the SOA
✗  Claim affiliation with Medicare/gov
✗  Use “best,” “free,” or false urgency
✗  Pressure same-call enrollment
✗  Skip required disclosures
✗  Misrepresent benefits or networks

## Slide 12
Key Knowledge Agents Must Master
Parts of Medicare
•  A – Hospital Insurance
•  B – Medical Insurance
•  C – Medicare Advantage
•  D – Prescription Drugs
•  Medigap – Supplements Original
Main Enrollment Periods
•  IEP – around 65th birthday
•  AEP – Oct 15 to Dec 7
•  MA OEP – Jan 1 to Mar 31
•  SEPs – qualifying life events
Original vs Advantage
•  Original: any provider accepting Medicare
•  Advantage: network, often extra benefits
•  Advantage has annual OOP max
•  Trade-offs must be stated clearly
Always Offer Alternatives
•  Medicare.gov
•  1-800-MEDICARE
•  Local SHIP counselor
•  Independent comparison tools
