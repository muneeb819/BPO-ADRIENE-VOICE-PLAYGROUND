## Slide 2
What is Medicare Advantage?
Medicare Advantage (Part C) is a private health plan alternative to Original Medicare (Parts A & B). These plans are offered by private insurance companies approved by Medicare and must cover everything Original Medicare covers — often with extra benefits.
Private Plans
Offered by Medicare-approved private insurers
All-in-One
Combines Part A, Part B, and usually Part D
Extra Benefits
Dental, vision, hearing, fitness & more

## Slide 3
Original Medicare vs. Medicare Advantage
Original Medicare
Part A (Hospital) + Part B (Medical)
No referral needed for specialists
Nationwide provider access
No out-of-pocket maximum
Optional Medigap + Part D
Higher flexibility, potentially higher costs
Medicare Advantage
All-in-one private plan (A + B + usually D)
Often requires network / referrals
Network-based (with some exceptions)
Annual out-of-pocket maximum
Extra benefits included
Lower predictability, more benefits

## Slide 4
Core Compliance Rules
Non-negotiable requirements for every marketing / sales / enrollment call
Identify Fully
State full name, licensed agent status, agency name. Disclose you are not affiliated with Medicare or the government.
Recording Consent
Advise the call is being recorded and obtain clear verbal permission before continuing.
TPMO Disclaimer
Deliver the required disclaimer about the number of organizations / plans you represent before discussing benefits.
Scope of Appointment
Document product types the beneficiary wants to discuss before any plan-specific conversation.
No Cold Calling
Only contact beneficiaries with documented prior permission. Never use purchased lists without consent.
No Pressure Language
Avoid “best,” “free,” false urgency, or any implication of government endorsement.

## Slide 7
Step 2 — Scope of Appointment (SOA)
Must be completed and documented before any plan-specific discussion.
SAMPLE SOA LANGUAGE
“Before we discuss any specific plans, I need to document the types of products you would like to review today. This is called a Scope of Appointment.
Would you like to discuss Medicare Advantage plans, Medicare Supplement plans, Part D Prescription Drug plans, or a combination?
Please note that the products we discuss today will be limited to what you authorize right now. You are under no obligation to enroll in anything.”
Limit Scope
Stay strictly within the product types authorized.
Document It
Verbal or electronic record is required.
No Obligation
Always remind the beneficiary there is no pressure to enroll.

## Slide 10
Steps 5–7 — Recap, Present, Close
5. Recap
•  Summarize doctors, meds, priorities, and budget concerns
•  Ask: “Did I get that right? Anything missing?”
•  Builds trust and prevents mis-matches
6. Present Options
•  Stay within the SOA products only
•  Present 1–3 relevant options factually
•  State trade-offs clearly (network, prior auth, costs)
•  Remind: “I do not offer every plan in your area”
7. Close / Next Steps
•  If ready: confirm understanding, complete enrollment
•  If not: offer summary + schedule follow-up
•  Never pressure same-call enrollment
•  End with contact info + Medicare.gov / 1-800-MEDICARE

Key Knowledge Agents Must Master

•  B – Medical Insurance

•  D – Prescription Drugs
•  Medigap – Supplements Original
Main Enrollment Periods
•  IEP – around 65th birthday
•  AEP – Oct 15 to Dec 7
•  MA OEP – Jan 1 to Mar 31

Original vs Advantage

## Slide 9
MEDICARE BASICS  ·  DETAILED OVERVIEW  ·  2026
FILLING THE GAPS
Medigap, Medicaid & Extra Help
Original Medicare leaves cost-sharing gaps. Several programs can reduce what you pay.
Medigap (Medicare Supplement)
Private policies that help pay deductibles, coinsurance, and copayments under Original Medicare. Standardized plans (A–N). Best bought during the 6-month Medigap Open Enrollment Period starting when Part B begins.
Medicaid / Dual Eligibility
State-federal program for people with limited income and resources. Dual eligibles can get help with Medicare premiums and cost-sharing, plus extra benefits such as long-term care and transportation.
Medicare Savings Programs
State programs that help pay Part A and/or Part B premiums and sometimes deductibles and coinsurance for people with modest income and resources.
Extra Help (Low-Income Subsidy)
Federal program that helps pay Part D premiums, deductibles, and copayments. Expanded eligibility under recent legislation. Apply through Social Security.
